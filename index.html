04 — Heritage

Art. Science.
Obsession.

"A car should be a work of art that happens to move at 350 km/h." Every Pagani begins as a sketch, not a spreadsheet — carved from carbon fibre and aerospace titanium inside a small atelier in San Cesario sul Panaro, Modena.

Italian Engineering

Founded by Horacio Pagani in 1992 after a decade at Lamborghini, the house fuses Renaissance craftsmanship with aerospace-grade material science — each chassis autoclave-cured in-house.

Absolute Craftsmanship

Every Zonda R takes over 2,600 hours to complete. No two carbon weaves are identical; no panel leaves the atelier until it is hand-finished to a mirror tolerance.
